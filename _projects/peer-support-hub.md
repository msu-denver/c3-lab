## P2Hub Is Live

The Peer Support Hub (P2Hub) opened to MSU Denver faculty on September 30, 2026, for the Learning Assistant (LA) application window. On October 28 the full LA workflow went live, and on November 2 the first LA decisions were released to faculty. Faculty, students and program coordinators sign in at [peersupporthub.app](https://peersupporthub.app) with their MSU Denver Microsoft accounts.

## About the Project

P2Hub is one platform that coordinates MSU Denver's three peer support programs: [Supplemental Instruction (SI)](https://www.msudenver.edu/student-academic-services/supplemental-instruction/), [Teaching Assistants (TA)](https://www.msudenver.edu/student-academic-services/teaching-assistant-program/), and [Learning Assistants (LA)](https://www.msudenver.edu/learning-assistant-program/). Faculty request peer support for their course sections in one place and rank the programs they prefer. Each program reviews on its own timeline and keeps control of its own rules, so P2Hub provides shared infrastructure, not central control.

The C3 Lab builds P2Hub in partnership with MSU Denver Student Academic Services. The Learning Assistant program is live today. Supplemental Instruction and Teaching Assistants are coming soon.

## What P2Hub Does Today

### One Request, Ranked Programs
Faculty request support for each course section once and rank their program choices. They can save a request part way through and finish it later. Requests reach each program when its own review window opens, so programs keep their established timelines.

### Faculty-First LA Hiring
Faculty choose how to staff a Learning Assistant position. They can name a student they already know, or open the position so students can apply. Faculty then review candidates and make offers themselves, inside P2Hub.

### Students Apply in One Place
Students find open LA positions and apply with their own application. Students whom faculty nominate accept the same program commitments as students who apply.

### Fair, Simultaneous Decisions
Coordinators review requests and hold their decisions until a set release moment. Every faculty member hears at the same time, so no one gets an early answer because of when their request happened to be reviewed.

### Everyone Stays Informed
Faculty and students get an email at each step, from submission to decision. Coordinators start each decision email from a template they can edit, and faculty who leave a request unsubmitted get a reminder before the window closes.

### Keeping Requests Accurate
After a decision, faculty can still update a section's meeting times, room and instructor of record, so programs always work from current information.

### Guides for Every Role
Step-by-step guides for students, faculty and LA coordinators are built into the site.

### Accessible by Design
P2Hub is built to Colorado's digital accessibility law (HB21-1110) and works on phones as well as desktops.

## The Challenge

Before P2Hub, MSU Denver's peer support programs ran through separate application processes, timelines and systems. Faculty filled out several forms with duplicate information and had no clear view of how support was distributed across their courses. Program staff lacked shared data, and the university had no coordinated way to evaluate effectiveness or make data-informed decisions about resource allocation.

Some courses could receive several forms of support while others, including those with high enrollment or high DFW rates, received none. That fragmentation made planning, equity and budgeting harder.

## Project Goals

- **Transparency:** Faculty, staff and leadership see consistent, timely information about peer support availability, deadlines and awards.
- **Equity:** A unified process and shared data help align peer support with student and course needs, reducing unintentional duplication and broadening access.
- **Coordination:** P2Hub streamlines the faculty experience without changing the mission or decision-making authority of SI, TA or LA. Each program keeps full control over its review process, timeline and eligibility criteria.

## Milestones

- Architecture design, workflow documentation and early interface prototypes
- Student developer onboarding

**Fall 2026:** Launch
- **September 30:** P2Hub opens to faculty for the LA application window
- **October 28:** The full LA workflow goes live, with coordinator review, student applications, faculty-first hiring and email at every step
- **November 2:** The first LA decisions are released to faculty, alongside an update shaped by coordinator feedback from launch
