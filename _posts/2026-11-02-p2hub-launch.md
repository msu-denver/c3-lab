The [Peer Support Hub (P2Hub)]({{ '/projects/peer-support-hub/' | relative_url }}) is live at [peersupporthub.app](https://peersupporthub.app). P2Hub is one platform for MSU Denver's three peer support programs: Supplemental Instruction, Teaching Assistants and Learning Assistants. The C3 Lab builds it in partnership with MSU Denver Student Academic Services.

P2Hub opened to faculty on September 30 for the Learning Assistant application window. On October 28 the full Learning Assistant workflow went live. Faculty request support and rank the programs they prefer, students apply to open positions, and faculty review candidates and make offers themselves. Coordinators hold their decisions until a set release moment, so every faculty member hears at the same time. On the morning of November 2, the first Learning Assistant decisions went out to faculty.

Each program keeps control of its own timeline and rules. P2Hub supplies the shared infrastructure: one place to request, apply, review and stay informed, with email at every step and step-by-step guides for every role. It is built to Colorado's digital accessibility law and works on phones as well as desktops.

The Spring 2027 term runs Learning Assistant applications through P2Hub. For Fall 2027, the goal is for the Teaching Assistant and Supplemental Instruction programs to take applications through P2Hub as well. P2Hub is built by C3 Lab student developers working alongside faculty, and it is the lab's newest platform in production.
